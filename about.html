Pikachu is a cute, yellow, mouse-like Pokémon with electric powers and is the mascot of the Pokémon world. Appearance Pikachu is a small, yellow Pokémon with long ears tipped in black, red cheeks that store electricity, and brown stripes on its back. Its tail is shaped like a lightning bolt, and female Pikachu have a heart-shaped end on their tails. Pikachu is about 40 centimeters (1 foot, 4 inches) tall and weighs around 6 kilograms (14 pounds) Kids encyclopedia Kids encyclopedia.
